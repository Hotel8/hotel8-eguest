# Hotel8:eGuest — Business Requirements & Functionality

> Audience: product owners, business analysts, and anyone evaluating what
> Hotel8:eGuest **does for the business**. For architecture read `README.md`;
> for wire-level detail read `DESIGN.md`.

## 1. Business Purpose

- Move the stay journey into the guest's hand: pre-arrival registration,
  mobile check-in, in-stay requests and messaging, and express check-out —
  reducing front-desk workload and queueing.
- Grow ancillary revenue by surfacing upsell offers (upgrades, early
  check-in, amenities) at the right journey moments with one-tap acceptance.
- Deepen loyalty engagement with points visibility and stay feedback, and
  feed property managers structured satisfaction data.
- Reduce payment friction: guests review their folio and settle outstanding
  balances before departure.

## 3. Business Capabilities

### Pre-arrival & check-in

| Capability | Business rule |
|---|---|
| Pre-check-in | The guest completes the registration card (identifiers, arrival time, special requests) before arrival; PMS retains it so front-desk interaction shrinks to key issuance. |
| Mobile check-in | Check-in executes against the PMS front office with the room assigned server-side; the guest sees the room only when the property releases it. |
| Digital key | Keys follow the property's policy: guest can activate/deactivate keys the PMS has issued — never fabricate a key. |

### In-stay

| Capability | Business rule |
|---|---|
| Service requests | Requests are typed (housekeeping, maintenance, amenities, wake-up) and tracked through their PMS lifecycle; the guest sees status, not staff internals. |
| Messaging | Threads are stay-scoped; front-desk replies appear in the same thread. |
| Upsell offers | Offers are generated by Hotel8:Upsell; accepting charges the guest via Payments and applies the upgrade in PMS; a declined offer is never re-presented by the client. |
| Room-service ordering | Orders come from the property's room-service menu and bind to the in-house stay (room + reservation resolve server-side — the guest never types a room number); prep states (sent/preparing/ready/served) drive the status stepper; the charge posts to the stay's folio via the POS check. |
| Rewards redemption | The catalog shows each reward's points cost and whether the member's balance covers it; redeeming exchanges points for a voucher code the guest presents at the desk — points never go negative. |
| Saved payment methods | Methods are token references (Payments tokenizes the instrument; the app stores the reference only — the guest never re-enters card numbers); a saved default covers folio quick-pay. |
| Folio review | The guest sees the authoritative folio (charges, payments, balance) — no local estimate. |
| Quick pay / express check-out | Payment covers the outstanding balance; check-out only completes when the folio is fully settled server-side. |

### Post-stay & profile

| Capability | Business rule |
|---|---|
| Feedback | A rating submits into the PMS feedback store; one rating per stay journey step. |
| Profile & preferences | The guest updates their own profile fields only; identity data is customer-env scoped. |
| Loyalty visibility | Members see balance and their transaction list; enrollment/earning rules live in Loyalty. |

## 4. Business Events (Outbound)

eGuest is a client; it emits no events. Guest actions trigger events in the
owning services (see their DESIGN docs):

| Business moment | Event family (owner) |
|---|---|
| Registration card stored / check-in executed | PMS front-office events |
| Service request created | PMS request events |
| Message exchanged | PMS messaging events |
| Digital key activated/revoked | PMS key events |
| Offer accepted/declined | Upsell offer events |
| Check-out posted / folio settled | PMS cashiering events |
| Feedback submitted | PMS feedback events |

**Inbound (notification-side):** the guest receives push/email notifications
produced by Sentin8:Post from those domain events (room ready, order/request
status).

## 5. Business Rules & Invariants

1. All guest traffic is BFF-proxied; the app cannot reach PMS, Upsell,
   Payments, or Loyalty directly.
2. A stay's data is visible only to its guest — server-side holder-scoping
   owns enforcement.
3. Keys are created/issued by the property; guests can only activate/stop
   issued keys.
4. An upsell acceptance routes through Payments; a decline is final until the
   service generates a new offer.
5. Check-out requires a fully settled folio; partial payment leaves the stay
   in-house.
6. Folio figures shown are the PMS folio, never a client-total.
7. Requests and messages are stay-scoped; a guest cannot read another
   stay's threads.

## 6. Target-User Roles & Use Cases

| Persona | What they do with eGuest |
|---|---|
| Hotel guest (pre-arrival) | Reviews stay, completes registration, accepts an upgrade. |
| Hotel guest (in-house) | Uses digital key, requests services, chats with front desk, reviews folio. |
| Hotel guest (departing) | Settles folio and checks out from the room, gets a receipt. |
| Loyalty member | Follows points earned from the stay and keeps profile preferences current. |
| Property manager | Benefits from reduced front-desk load and structured feedback data. |

## 7. KPIs / Success Criteria

- **Share of check-ins via mobile/kiosk** vs front desk.
- **Upsell acceptance rate** and ancillary revenue per stay.
- **Request resolution visibility** — guests see status without calling the
  desk (reduce inbound calls).
- **Express check-out share** and folio settlement timing (fewer depart-day
  queues).
- **Feedback coverage** (rating submission share) and satisfaction trend.
- **Security:** zero key activations outside PMS-issued keys; zero cross-stay
  data exposures.

## 9. Acceptance Criteria

1. A guest completes pre-check-in and then checks in on the phone, seeing the
   assigned room and (if enabled) an active digital key.
2. A request submitted in-stay appears in the PMS staff queue and its status
   is visible in the app.
3. A message thread shows guest and front-desk messages in order for the
   current stay.
4. An accepted upsell charges the guest and reflects in the folio.
5. A guest checks out only after their folio balance reaches zero, and the
   stay moves to departed in PMS.
6. Feedback submitted for a stay is recorded with the guest's identity and
   the stay reference.
7. All guest calls are `/proxy/*`; no backend endpoint appears in traces.

## 10. Open Business Questions

- Does room-service ordering ride the POS proxy menu contract, and which
  revenue center owns delivery?
- Rewards catalog + redemption screens: Loyalty API surface timing.
- Mobile-key handoff per lock vendor: BLE and NFC both, or property choice?
- Push notification depth: which journey events notify, and when is email
  preferred?
